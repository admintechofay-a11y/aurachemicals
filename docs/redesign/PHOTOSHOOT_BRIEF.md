# Commercial Photoshoot Brief & Asset Specification — Aura Chemicals

> **Document Version:** 1.0.0  
> **Target Entity:** Aura Space Infra Private Limited (trading as Aura Chemicals)  
> **Brand Direction:** Laboratory Editorial & Industrial Synthesis  
> **Purpose:** Commissioning authentic, high-resolution photography of physical warehousing, packaging forms, dispatch fleets, analytical testing, and corporate facilities to replace legacy generic stock imagery.

---

## 1. Objective & Aesthetic Mandate

Aura Chemicals is an established B2B chemical distributor and manufacturer based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, operating continuously since 2014. Commercial procurement officers evaluate chemical suppliers on **operational authenticity, material traceability, and regulatory discipline**.

### Absolute Aesthetic Rules:
- **Zero generic stock models:** No smiling models in lab coats pretending to hold beakers.
- **Material honesty:** Photograph actual warehouse bays, ISO tanks, IBC totes, HDPE drums, multi-wall paper bags, and genuine laboratory QC test benches.
- **Lighting & Color Grading:** Natural, neutral daylight or crisp clean industrial lighting. Avoid artificial dramatic colored gels or oversaturated filters. Preserve the true color of chemical compounds, drums, and packaging.
- **Resolution & Delivery:** Minimum 4K resolution (3840×2160 or higher) in uncompressed RAW + sRGB Web-optimized JPEG/WebP formats.

## 2. Priority Shot List (6 Core Operational Themes)

### Category A: Chemical Warehousing & Bulk Storage Facilities (8–10 Shots)
1. **Wide Warehouse Interior:** Clean, well-lit chemical storage bay showing pallet racking, forklift lanes, and organized inventory tags.
2. **IBC Tote Racks:** Neatly stacked 1,000L Intermediate Bulk Containers (IBCs) with clear identification placards and batch labels.
3. **HDPE Drum Line:** Clean row of 200L/250kg industrial HDPE blue drums with tamper-evident bung seals and hazard warning diamonds.
4. **Multi-wall Bag Pallets:** Neatly shrink-wrapped pallets of 25kg/50kg multi-wall paper and HDPE woven bags stored on wooden or plastic pallets.
5. **Temperature-Controlled Storage:** Cold room / climate-controlled chemical bay showing digital temperature readouts and humidity monitoring.

### Category B: Industrial Packaging & Tamper-Evident Closures (6–8 Shots)
1. **Close-up of Tamper-Evident Seals:** Detailed macro shot of factory drum seals, heat-sealed bag liners, and serialized security tags.
2. **Batch & Label Detail:** Close-up of standard GHS/CLP compliant label showing Chemical Name, Grade (IP/BP/USP), CAS Number, Batch No, Mfg Date, Expiry Date, and Net Weight.
3. **Compound Morphology (Selected Non-Hazardous Lines):** Macro photography of pharmaceutical-grade crystalline powders (e.g. Paracetamol crystals, Citric Acid monohydrate, Sodium Acetate flakes) photographed cleanly in laboratory glass Petri dishes.

### Category C: Analytical QC & Quality Assurance Laboratory (6–8 Shots)
1. **Analytical Test Bench:** Authentic bench showing calibrated laboratory equipment (digital analytical balances, pH meters, Karl Fischer moisture titrators, HPLC or spectrophotometer).
2. **Sample Retain Archive:** Systematic shelf or cabinet holding sealed 100g/250g reference retain samples in amber glass or HDPE bottles with dated labels.
3. **CoA Review / Documentation Desk:** Physical Certificate of Analysis (CoA) verification paperwork alongside analytical instruments (no generic staged smiling).

### Category D: Material Handling, Logistics & Dispatch Fleet (6–8 Shots)
1. **Loading Bay Activity:** Forklift loading palletized chemical drums or bags onto dispatch trucks or container lorries.
2. **Dedicated Fleet / Tanker:** Dedicated chemical tanker truck or clean cargo vehicle branded or staged at the dispatch terminal with hazchem safety boards.
3. **Dispatch Staging Area:** Quality-cleared orders tagged for pharmaceutical, agrochemical, or industrial formulators.

### Category E: Corporate Office & Management Team (4–6 Shots)
1. **Executive Management Portraits:** Natural, professional headshots of Directors and Key Technical Officers at desks or conference settings (clean, unpretentious business formal/semi-formal).
2. **Commercial Desk / Operations Room:** Operations and documentation team processing shipping dockets, customs papers, and pharmacopeia dossiers.

## 4. Photographer Onboarding & Safety Brief

1. **PPE Mandatory:** Photographer and assistant must wear certified steel-toe safety shoes, high-visibility vest, safety glasses, and hard hat in active warehouse and loading zones.
2. **Explosion-Proof / Hazardous Zone Rules:** Ensure camera flashes and lighting battery packs adhere to facility safety clearance before entering volatile solvent transfer zones.
3. **Commercial Confidentiality:** Do not photograph proprietary formulations, internal customer shipping labels containing client PII, or competitor product barcodes.
