## 1. Audiences & Their Jobs-to-be-Done

### A. Procurement Head / Sourcing Director (Pharma & Formulation Companies)
- **Context:** Formulating tablets, syrups, or injectables requiring strict regulatory compliance under DCGI / CDSCO and international pharmacopeias.
- **Job-to-be-Done:** Quickly verify that Aura is an established, legitimate supplier; locate an Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API) by chemical name or CAS registry number; confirm pharmacopeial standard (IP, BP, USP, EP, JP); and submit an RFQ with specific volume, grade, and documentation requests (CoA, MSDS, TSE/BSE declarations).
- **Core Anxieties:** Impurity profile, regulatory non-compliance, inconsistent batch quality, delayed dispatch shutting down manufacturing lines.

### B. Plant / Purchase Manager (Industrial Manufacturing & Chemical Processing)
- **Context:** Operating industrial facilities consuming bulk solvents (MEG, DMF, Toluene, Acetone, Ethyl Acetate, IPA), acids, or manufacturing phosphates.
- **Job-to-be-Done:** Confirm product availability, bulk packaging configurations (drums, ISO tanks, IBCs, tanker loads), commercial terms, and prompt dispatch logistics to manufacturing sites across Gujarat and India.
- **Core Anxieties:** Supply chain interruptions, volatile pricing, stockouts, non-compliant packaging.

### C. Chemical Trader / Regional Distributor / Export Buyer
- **Context:** Sourcing wholesale lots or consolidated containers for regional redistribution or overseas supply.
- **Job-to-be-Done:** Review breadth of portfolio (in-house phosphates, direct China imports, domestic agencies), verify origin and make, and initiate direct commercial negotiations via phone or WhatsApp.
- **Core Anxieties:** Margin viability, delivery schedules, documentation authenticity.

### D. Engineering & Quality Assurance Manager
- **Context:** Managing plant assets, pressure vessels, pipelines, and structural fabrication requiring statutory or risk-based inspection.
- **Job-to-be-Done:** Evaluate Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) capabilities (UT, RT, MPT, DPT), RBI methodologies, welding inspection credentials, and understand engagement scope and deliverables (formal inspection reports).
- **Core Anxieties:** Inspector qualification, equipment calibration accuracy, compliance with ASME/API/ASTM codes.

### E. Principal Partners, Institutional Stakeholders & Career Seekers
- **Context:** Major manufacturers evaluating distribution capability; banks, audit authorities, and prospective employees assessing legitimacy.
- **Job-to-be-Done:** Verify corporate registration (ROC Ahmedabad), track record since 2014, compliance posture, and operational scale.
- **Core Anxieties:** Reputational risk, financial stability, operational governance.

## 2. Conversion Architecture

### Primary Conversion Funnel
1. **Commercial RFQ (Request for Quote) Submission:**
   - Multi-item quotation builder (RFQ Basket) allowing buyers to specify Product, CAS, Pharmacopeia / Grade, Target Quantity & Unit, Delivery Destination, and Timeline.
   - Guaranteed server-side persistence, real transaction reference ID, and email notification to the sales desk.
   - One-click alternative: pre-filled WhatsApp quotation inquiry connecting directly to `+91 7220000877`.

### Secondary Conversions
- **Technical Document Request:** Requesting Certificates of Analysis (CoA), Technical Data Sheets (TDS), and Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS/SDS).
- **Sample Request:** Commercial pilot sample requisition for lab evaluation and quality testing.
- **Direct Desk Call:** Click-to-call direct connection to management desk.
- **Inspection Service Engagement:** Technical inspection scope requisition for plant NDT or RBI testing.

## 4. Tone of Voice & Content Principles

- **Precise & Mathematical:** Use exact chemical nomenclature, verified CAS numbers, correct molecular formulas ($H_2SO_4$, $Na_3PO_4$), and clear packaging quantities (kg, MT, IBC tanks).
- **Calm & Authoritative:** Avoid hype, buzzwords, and marketing puffery. Banned words: *"world-class"*, *"revolutionary"*, *"game-changing"*, *"unrivaled"*, *"best-in-class"*.
- **Materially Honest:** Only state what can be evidenced. If an assay specification or lead time is not confirmed in writing by the owner, indicate that specifications are quoted against the specific manufacturer's Certificate of Analysis.
- **Direct Action-Oriented:** Action verbs paired with concrete nouns: *"Request a Quote"*, *"Add to RFQ"*, *"Download Product Sheet"*, *"Search Catalog by Name or CAS"*.

4. **Accessibility:** 100% compliance with WCAG 2.2 Level AA (Zero critical, serious, or moderate axe violations).
5. **SEO Indexation:** 100% pre-rendered static HTML coverage across all product and sector routes.
